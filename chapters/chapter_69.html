69 Thread-Leaved Sandwort

Names

Common name – Thread-Leaved Sandwort

Scientific name – Arenaria capillaris

General Information

Thread-Leaved Sandwort ( Arenaria capillaris ) is a small perennial plant with hair-like leaves and small white flowers, native to western North America. [1] However, there is no specific documented traditional medicinal uses of this particular species. [2] There is also limited ethnobotanical research on this specific species. [2]

Any medicinal applications should be considered theoretical and potentially unsafe. Traditional plant medicines should only be used under guidance from qualified practitioners familiar with both the plants and traditional preparation methods.
